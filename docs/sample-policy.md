# Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

# Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

# Travel and Expenses
Meals are reimbursed up to 50 USD per day while travelling on company business. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

# Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Company laptops must use the VPN when accessing internal systems.
